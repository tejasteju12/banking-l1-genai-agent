# SOP-005

Title: Suspicious or Unauthorized Transaction
Category: Security
Issue: Suspicious Transaction
Support Level: L1
Version: 1.0
Status: Active

## Symptoms

Customer reports a transaction they do not recognize.

Examples:

- Unknown transaction
- Unauthorized payment
- Suspicious card transaction
- Unknown UPI transaction
- Account may be compromised

## Resolution Steps

### Step 1

Treat the report as a potential security incident.

### Step 2

Do not ask the customer for:

- OTP
- PIN
- Password
- CVV
- Full card credentials

### Step 3

Use the approved customer authentication process.

### Step 4

Check the transaction through the authorized banking support system.

### Step 5

Follow the bank's approved fraud/dispute procedure.

### Step 6

If required by bank policy, initiate appropriate card/account blocking through authorized systems.

## Escalation

Immediately escalate to the fraud/security team when:

- Unauthorized transaction is confirmed.
- Customer reports account compromise.
- Multiple suspicious transactions are present.
- Customer's credentials may have been compromised.
- L1 does not have authority to perform required security actions.

## Security

Never attempt to resolve a suspected fraud case using an unapproved process.

Do not expose sensitive customer information.
